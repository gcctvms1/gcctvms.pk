Why Live Monitoring

Recording is not the same as monitoring

A mobile viewing app lets you check your cameras yourself, which helps only when you are looking at your phone. Live monitoring puts a person on the screen whose job is to watch. Your security no longer depends on whether you happened to open the app at 3 a.m.

In Pakistan, some companies use the phrase remote monitoring to mean setting up app access for the owner. On this website it means people watching your feeds for you.

Power, internet and live monitoring

Remote monitoring needs a live video connection. During load-shedding or an internet outage, cameras, recorders and routers without backup power go offline, and nobody can watch a feed that is not there. Keeping the recorder, the cameras and the internet router on a UPS helps keep your site visible when the power goes.
