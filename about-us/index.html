About GCCTVMS Pakistan

Global CCTV Monitoring Services (GCCTVMS) is a remote CCTV monitoring company. gcctvms.pk is its Pakistan website and covers the services GCCTVMS offers to clients in Pakistan. The company's international website is gcctvms.com.

Our Work

What we do

We watch camera feeds remotely and alert clients when something needs their attention. In Pakistan that covers 24/7 live monitoring, virtual guarding, a virtual doorman, a virtual receptionist and nanny cam monitoring.

Our Story

How GCCTVMS started

GCCTVMS was founded in 2019 by Najmul Hasan Sayed. The company was built on the idea that cameras should help stop incidents while they are happening, rather than only record them for review afterwards.
